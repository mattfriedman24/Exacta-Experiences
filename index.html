about exacta experiences

We started Exacta because getting out and exploring LA shouldn't require sitting in traffic — or going it alone.

our story

Exacta Experiences was born out of a simple observation: Los Angeles has world-class museums, gardens, restaurants, symphony halls, and cultural destinations — but getting to them from the suburbs is a barrier for many people, especially those who'd rather not drive on the freeway. We built Exacta to solve that. We plan the activities, reserve the tickets, book the restaurants, and provide the shuttle — all you have to do is show up at your neighborhood pickup spot and enjoy the day.

who we're for

Exacta Experiences is designed for adults 55+ living in the Encino, Tarzana, Woodland Hills, and Thousand Oaks areas who want to stay active, explore, and connect with others — without the stress of driving into the city. Every outing is a paired experience: two activities together. Small groups of 8–10 mean you actually get to know the people you're with.

how it works

1
browse upcoming trips
Daytime outings, evening events, wine tastings, symphony nights — all curated and planned in advance.
2
reserve your spot
Book online for yourself or a partner. Spots are limited to 8–10 per trip. Pay securely at checkout.
3
show up at your pickup spot
Meet the shuttle at your local hub in Encino or Thousand Oaks. No freeway, no parking, no stress.
4
enjoy the day — or night
Your guide handles everything. Relax, explore, and meet great people along the way.

pricing

Daytime outings start at $50 per person or $85 for two. Evening experiences vary by event and are always clearly listed before you book. Our shuttle pricing is flat — no surge, no surprises, and often less than what you'd spend on rideshare alone.
